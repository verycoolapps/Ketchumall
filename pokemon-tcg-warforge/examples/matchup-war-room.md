> "I'm playing Dragapult ex this weekend. What's my matchup vs Marnie's Grimmsnarl? Give me a full game plan."

## WARFORGE RESPONSE

### MATCHUP IDENTIFICATION

**Our deck:** Dragapult ex (aggro, fast attacker, psychic type)
**Opponent deck:** Marnie's Grimmsnarl (control/disruption, dark type)

**Archetype matchup:** Aggro vs Control/Disruption

### ARCHETYPE ANALYSIS

**Dragapult ex:**
- Win condition: Fast KO with Dragapult ex, take prizes quickly
- Strengths: Speed, damage output, consistency
- Weaknesses: Fragile (2-prize liability), vulnerable to disruption

**Marnie's Grimmsnarl:**
- Win condition: Disrupt our setup, control board, win late
- Strengths: Hand disruption, item denial, resource denial
- Weaknesses: Slow setup, vulnerable to fast aggro

### GAME PLAN OVERVIEW

This is a **race matchup**. Dragapult needs to set up and attack before Grimmsnarl stabilizes. Grimmsnarl needs to disrupt Dragapult's setup and win the long game.

**Key question:** Can Dragapult set up by turn 2-3, or does Grimmsnarl disrupt first?

### EARLY GAME (Turns 1-2)

**Our priorities:**
- Set up Dragapult ex as fast as possible
- Attach energy every turn
- Don't overextend into their disruption
- Keep options open (don't commit to one line)

**Their priorities:**
- Disrupt our hand (Marnie's ability, hand disruption cards)
- Deny our items (Crushing Hammer, item denial)
- Set up Grimmsnarl for disruption
- Don't attack early — survive our aggression

**Key interaction:** Who sets up first? If Dragapult gets attacker + energy by turn 2, we win race. If Grimmsnarl disrupts our hand/items, we struggle.

### MID GAME (Turns 3-5)

**Our priorities:**
- Get Dragapult ex attacking every turn
- Use Boss to KO their threats
- Manage resources carefully (don't waste supporters)
- Trade efficiently — don't let them take easy prizes

**Their priorities:**
- Complete their disruption engine
- Start attacking with Grimmsnarl
- Deny our recovery options
- Win the resource war

**Key interaction:** Their disruption vs our consistency. If we can draw through their disruption, we win. If they cut our options, we lose.

### LATE GAME (Turns 6+)

**Our priorities:**
- Close the game before they stabilize
- Conserve resources for final push
- Don't let them deck us out
- Take remaining prizes efficiently

**Their priorities:**
- Win the resource war (they're ahead if game goes long)
- Deck us out or win by prizes
- Use their disruption to close out

**Key interaction:** Who has more resources left? If we raced ahead, we win. If they controlled, they win.

### PRIZE MAPPING

**Their key targets:**
- Dragapult ex (2 prizes when KO'd — big target)
- Our setup Pokémon (if they can KO them)
- Our bench (if they have spread damage)

**Our key targets:**
- Grimmsnarl (if it's their main attacker)
- Their setup Pokémon (if disrupting their engine)
- Their bench (if we have spread damage)

**2-prize vs 1-prize math:**
- Dragapult ex gives up 2 prizes when KO'd — risky
- Grimmsnarl likely gives up 2 prizes (if ex/V) or 1 (if regular)
- We need to trade efficiently — don't let them take 2 for 1

**Gust targets:**
- Boss their Grimmsnarl when it's attacking
- Boss their setup Pokémon if they're key to engine
- Don't Boss too early — wait for max value

### DAMAGE THRESHOLDS

**We need to deal:**
- Enough damage to KO their threats in 1-2 hits
- Dragapult ex typically hits 200+ damage
- Need to OHKO or 2HKO their key attackers

**They need to deal:**
- Enough damage to KO our Dragapult ex
- Grimmsnarl damage varies — check their list
- If they OHKO Dragapult, we lose tempo

**OHKO potential:**
- Us: Yes, Dragapult ex hits hard
- Them: Depends on their damage output

**2HKO potential:**
- Us: Yes, consistent 2HKO on most targets
- Them: Likely yes on our 2-prize Dragapult

### DISRUPTION OPPORTUNITIES

**Their vulnerable windows:**
- Turn 1-2: Setting up, haven't stabilized
- When they need specific cards (evolution pieces, energy)
- When they're about to attack (Boss timing)

**Our disruption tools:**
- Boss's Orders (gust their threats)
- Crushing Hammer (energy denial)
- Hand disruption (if we run it)

**Their disruption tools:**
- Marnie's ability (hand disruption)
- Item denial (Crushing Hammer, etc.)
- Resource denial (slow our setup)

**Counter-disruption:**
- Draw through their disruption (Professor's, Cynthia)
- Boss their key attackers before they disrupt
- Save resources for when they try to close

### DANGEROUS OPPONENT PLAYS

🚨 **Marnie's ability on our Dragapult** — discards key cards, slows our setup
🚨 **Item denial on our Ultra Ball** — can't find evolution pieces
🚨 **Grimmsnarl OHKO on our Dragapult** — we lose 2 prizes, they lose 1-2
🚨 **Deck-out race** — if game goes long, they win on resources

### COMMON MISTAKES (OUR SIDE)

✗ **Overextending into their disruption** — don't put all eggs in one basket
✗ **Attacking too early for small damage** — wait for max damage
✗ **Not using Boss at right time** — save for their key attacker
✗ **Wasting supporters** — don't draw when you don't need to
✗ **Ignoring their disruption** — adapt when they cut your options

### RECOVERY PLAN

**If behind:**
- Go for broke — take risks, try to close fast
- Boss their biggest threat every turn
- Don't defend — you need to race
- Save disruption for their key turns

**If disrupted:**
- Find alternate lines (different attackers, different resources)
- Don't panic — adapt to their disruption
- Conserve what you have — don't waste resources
- Look for comeback opportunities (their mistakes, bad draws)

### DIFFICULTY ASSESSMENT

**Rating:** NEUTRAL to slightly UNFAVORABLE
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (theory-based, no gameplay data)

**Justification:**
- Dragapult's speed helps vs Grimmsnarl's slow setup
- But Grimmsnarl's disruption hurts Dragapult's consistency
- Matchup depends on who sets up first
- Tech choices matter (more draw vs more disruption)

**How to improve:**
- Add more draw supporters (Professor's, Cynthia)
- Add energy acceleration (Chien-Pao)
- Add hand disruption (Judge, Xerosicclaw)
- Practice Boss timing — when to gust vs when to hold

### KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **Race matchup** — who sets up first wins
2. **Disruption is key** — Grimmsnarl disrupts, you must adapt
3. **Boss timing matters** — save for their key attacker
4. **Don't overextend** — preserve options for comeback
5. **Practice the matchup** — theory is good, experience is better

This matchup is winnable with proper play and tech choices. Don't underestimate Grimmsnarl's disruption.
