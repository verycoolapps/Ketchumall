### CARD-BY-CARD EXPLANATION

**Riolu (4)** — Basic fighting starter. Evolve into Lucario. 4 for consistency.

**Lucario (4)** — Stage 1 evolution. Bridge to Lucario ex. 4 for reliable evolution.

**Lucario ex (4)** — Main attacker. Fighting type counters Dragapult's psychic weakness. Big damage, 2-prize liability. 4 for consistent access.

**Kirlia (3)** — Draw engine. Ability to draw when played. 3 for consistency without over-diluting.

**Manaphy (3)** — Consistency + healing. Provides support for Lucario. 3 for flexibility.

**Professor's Research (4)** — Draw 7, discard 6. Core consistency engine.

**Boss's Orders (4)** — Gust opponent's threats. Key disruption + KO enabler.

**Cynthia (3)** — Search deck for Pokémon + Energy. Finds evolution pieces and energy.

**Iono (2)** — Alternative supporter for flexibility. Draw or search depending on need.

**Judge (1)** — Hand disruption. Tech for combo/mirror matchups.

**Ultra Ball (4)** — Discard 2, search Pokémon. Consistency + evolution finder.

**Poké Pad (4)** — Search deck for Pokémon. Simple consistency.

**Switch (2)** — Swap Active with Bench. Mobility + protection.

**Crushing Hammer (3)** — Discard energy from opponent. Tech for energy-dependent decks (Dragapult, Charizard).

**Xerosicclaw (1)** — ACE SPEC. Discard hand from opponent. Tech for combo/control matchups.

**Fighting Energy (8)** — Lucario needs 2 Fighting Energy. 8 for consistent attachment.

### STRATEGY BRIEF

**Primary win condition:** Lucario ex attacks for big damage, take prizes.

**Game plan vs Dragapult:**
- Lucario's fighting type counters Dragapult's psychic
- Set up Lucario fast, attack before they set up Dragapult
- Use Crushing Hammer to slow their energy attachment

**Game plan vs Grimmsnarl:**
- Out-resource their disruption
- Boss their key attackers when they try to set up
- Don't overextend into their board clear

**Game plan vs Charizard:**
- Trade efficiently — both are ex (2 prizes)
- Boss their Charizard when it's biggest threat
- Save Crushing Hammer for their energy

### SIDEBOARD/TECH CONSIDERATIONS

**For Dragapult-heavy meta:**
- Consider +1 Crushing Hammer, -1 Judge
- Consider +1 Fighting Energy, -1 supporter

**For control-heavy meta:**
- Consider +1 Boss's Orders, -1 Ultra Ball
- Consider +1 healing/support card

**For combo-heavy meta:**
- Consider +1 Judge or Xerosicclaw
- Consider +1 Boss for disruption timing

### TOURNAMENT PREPARATION

**Pre-tournament:**
- Test vs Dragapult ex deck (most popular)
- Test vs Grimmsnarl deck (second most popular)
- Test vs Charizard ex deck (big hitter)
- Practice sideboard decisions

**Match day:**
- Game 1: Learn their deck, don't reveal tech
- Games 2-3: Adjust sideboard for their deck
- Track their key cards, patterns, weaknesses

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR COMPETITIVE PLAYERS

1. **Meta awareness matters** — build for what people play
2. **Counter-meta > best deck** — exploit popular decks' weaknesses
3. **Tech cards win games** — Boss, Crushing Hammer, Judge are key
4. **Sideboard strategy** — adjust between games, don't over-sideboard
5. **Test your matchups** — theory is good, practice is better
