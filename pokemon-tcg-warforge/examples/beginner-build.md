### CARD-BY-CARD EXPLANATION

**Charmander (4)** — Basic Fire starter. Evolve into Charmeleon. Run 4 for consistency — you want to see one in opening hand.

**Charmeleon (4)** — Stage 1 evolution. Bridge between Charmander and Charizard. 4 copies for reliable evolution.

**Charizard ex (4)** — Main attacker. Big damage, 2-prize liability but worth it. Run 4 for consistent access.

**Pidgey (2)** — Basic draw engine. When played, search deck for Pokémon. Simple consistency.

**Pidgeotto (2)** — Stage 1 draw engine. Better draw effect than Pidgey. 2 copies for backup.

**Professor's Research (4)** — Draw 7, discard 6. Consistency engine. Run max for reliability.

**Boss's Orders (4)** — Gust opponent's Pokémon to Active. Key for KO'ing their threats. Run max.

**Cynthia (2)** — Search deck for Pokémon + Energy. Helps find evolution pieces. 2 for flexibility.

**Ultra Ball (4)** — Discard 2, search deck for Pokémon. Consistency + evolution finder. Run max.

**Poké Pad (4)** — Search deck for Pokémon. Simple consistency. Run max.

**Switch (2)** — Swap Active with Bench. Mobility + retreat alternative. 2 for flexibility.

**Fire Energy (10)** — Charizard needs 2 Fire Energy to attack. 10 ensures consistent attachment.

### STRATEGY BRIEF

**Primary win condition:** Charizard ex attacks for big damage, take prizes.

**Setup sequence:**
- Turn 1: Play Charmander, attach Fire Energy, Pidgey for draw
- Turn 2: Evolve to Charmeleon, attach more energy, draw cards
- Turn 3: Evolve to Charizard ex, attack for big damage

**Key idea:** Get Charizard ex set up by turn 3, then attack every turn for prizes.

### MATCHUP NOTES

- **vs Aggro:** Race to set up Charizard first. If they KO you before turn 3, you lose.
- **vs Control:** Apply pressure early, don't let them stabilize.
- **vs Combo:** Disrupt with Boss when they're setting up.

### PROBABILITY CHECK

**Opening hand (7 cards):**
- P(at least 1 Charmander) ≈ 65%
- P(at least 1 evolution piece) ≈ 70%
- P(at least 1 Fire Energy) ≈ 75%

**Turn 3 Charizard:**
- With 4x each stage + search cards, ~75% chance to have full line by turn 3

### PLAY GUIDE

**Opening priorities:**
- Keep hands with Charmander + Fire Energy + any trainer
- Mulligan if no Basic Pokémon or no way to attack

**First turn:**
- Active Charmander
- Attach Fire Energy
- Play Pidgey for draw if available
- Use Ultra Ball/Poké Pad to find evolution pieces

**Mid game:**
- Evolve to Charmeleon turn 2, Charizard ex turn 3
- Attach energy every turn
- Attack when you have 2+ energy
- Use Boss to gust their threats

**Common mistakes:**
- Evolving too early before having energy
- Attacking too early for small damage
- Not using Boss to remove their biggest threat

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR BEGINNERS

1. **Consistency > Power** — 4x copies of key cards beat 1x powerful cards
2. **Simple engines work** — Pidgey draw is easier than complex combos
3. **Know your plan** — Charizard by turn 3, attack every turn
4. **Boss is your friend** — gust their threats, take prizes
5. **Don't overcomplicate** — learn fundamentals first

This deck teaches: evolution, energy attachment, supporter usage, prize taking, and basic strategy.
