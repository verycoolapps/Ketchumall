# 🎮 POKÉMON TCG FUNDAMENTALS

> Core game mechanics, card taxonomy, and format knowledge. Load when reasoning about rules, legality, or card types.

---

## 1. CARD TAXONOMY

### Pokémon
- **Basic Pokémon** — no evolution requirement; played to Bench or as Active.
- **Stage 1 / Stage 2** — evolve from previous stage; keep damage/counters/status.
- **Pokémon ex** — high-impact, gives up **2 Prize cards** when KO'd. Rule Box applies.
- **Pokémon V / VMAX / VSTAR** — V gives up 2 prizes; VSTAR 2 prizes (modern rules); VMAX typically 3 prizes when KO'd (check current rules — mechanics change across eras).
- **Tera Pokémon** — mechanic introduced in Terastal Festival era; check current legality.
- **Ancient / Future / etc.** — classification tags, not mechanics by themselves.

### Abilities & Attacks
- **Ability** — passive or once-per-turn effect (unless stated otherwise).
- **Attack** — costs energy, deals damage, may have effects (discard, status, bench damage, heal, etc.).
- **Retreat Cost** — energy to pay for switching Active Pokémon.
- **Weakness** — ×2 damage from specific type (unless resistance applies — weakness applies BEFORE resistance in damage calc).
- **Resistance** — −30 damage from specific type (modern: −30; older eras differed — VERIFY current rules).

### Trainer Cards
- **Supporter** — one per turn limit (modern rule).
- **Item** — fast effect, generally usable multiple per turn.
- **Stadium** — affects both players; replaced when new one played.
- **Pokémon Tool** — attaches to a Pokémon; one per Pokémon (modern).
- **Special Energy** — provides effects beyond color.
- **Technical Machine (TM)** — item that grants an attack/effect to compatible Pokémon.
- **ACE SPEC** — max 1 copy in deck total (all ACE SPECs combined).

### Other Zones
- **Prize cards** — 6 face-down cards; take 1 for regular KO, 2 for ex/V/VMAX per current rules.
- **Lost Zone** — cards removed from game entirely (some effects put cards here).
- **Discard pile**, **Deck**, **Hand**, **Bench** (max 5 modern).

## 2. CORE MECHANICS

- **Evolution** — Stage 1 onto matching Basic, Stage 2 onto matching Stage 1. Cannot evolve same turn played (unless card says otherwise). Evolution is NOT a retreated/returned state — keeps damage.
- **Attaching Energy** — one per turn per Pokémon from hand (unless effects allow more).
- **Retreating** — pay retreat cost, switch Active with Bench Pokémon.
- **Knock Out (KO)** — HP reaches 0; take prizes per card's rule.
- **Special Conditions** — Burned, Poisoned, Asleep, Paralyzed, Confused (verify current condition list — mechanics evolve).
- **Deck construction** — 60 cards typical; max 4 copies per card name (Basic Energy unlimited); format-specific rules apply.

## 3. FORMATS & LEGALITY

| Format | Scope | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Most recent ~3-4 sets + current rotation | Regulation marks D/E/F/G... rotate annually |
| **Expanded** | Wider card pool, older mechanics | Black & White era forward typically |
| **Unlimited / Other** | Only if explicitly stated | State assumptions |

**Always verify:**
- Regulation mark legality for the format
- Set rotation status
- ACE SPEC limits
- Copy limits (4 max, energies exempt)
- Tournament-specific rules (Play! Pokémon, locals vs regionals vs worlds)

**Physical TCG vs Pokémon TCG Live** — different card pools, different legality, different mechanics timing. NEVER assume parity. Always ask or state which environment.

---

## 4. WIN CONDITIONS

- **Deck Out** — a player unable to draw when required loses.
- **All Prizes Taken** — collect all 6 prize cards.
- **No Pokémon** — cannot field a Pokémon when required.

Most games end via prizes. Deck-out is a real threat in long control games.

---

## 5. TURN STRUCTURE (simplified modern)

1. Flip coin if going first (modern: first player cannot attack T1 — verify current rule).
2. Draw a card.
3. Main phase: attach energy, evolve, play trainers, use abilities, retreat, attack.
4. End turn.

**Key timing:** Abilities trigger at defined times; attacks resolve fully; stadium effects continuous.

---

## 6. COMMON PITFALLS

- Forgetting Rule Box restrictions on ex/V/VMAX.
- Miscounting prize values (ex = 2, VMAX = 3 in most eras).
- Applying weakness before resistance incorrectly (order: weakness → damage → resistance).
- Assuming older mechanic text applies to current prints (errata exist).
- Mixing up "can't attack" vs "can't use abilities" effects.
- Forgetting Supporter once-per-turn limit.
- Ignoring stadium replacement timing.
