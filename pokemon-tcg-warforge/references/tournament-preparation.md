## MATCH DAY PROTOCOL

### Round Structure (Best-of-Three)
- Game 1: No sideboarding (usually) — establish game plan
- Games 2-3: Sideboard adjustments allowed — tech cards in/out
- Sideboard strategy: bring in counters for THEIR deck, not generic answers

### Between Games
- [ ] Assess what worked/didn't in previous game
- [ ] Identify opponent's key cards and patterns
- [ ] Adjust sideboard (if applicable)
- [ ] Mental reset — don't carry frustration forward
- [ ] Hydrate, breathe, refocus

### Time Management
- [ ] Know round time limit (usually 50 min for Bo3)
- [ ] Track time remaining each game
- [ ] Don't stall — make decisions, accept variance
- [ ] If behind on time, simplify play (fewer complex lines)
- [ ] If ahead on time, can afford more calculation

## IN-MATCH DECISION FRAMEWORK

### Opening Decisions
- **Keep or mulligan?** — assess against matchup's speed
- **Which Basic to active?** — matchup-dependent (see matchup-analysis.md)
- **What to search first?** — consistency vs disruption timing

### Mid-Game Decisions
- **Attack or set up?** — depends on damage race math
- **Boss now or later?** — disruption timing vs information
- **Supporter timing** — when to draw/search vs when to hold
- **Stadium timing** — when to play/deny

### Late-Game Decisions
- **Push for game or stabilize?** — risk assessment
- **Conserve resources or spend?** — deck-out awareness
- **Tech card usage** — save for critical moment or use early?

## MATCHUP-SPECIFIC PREPARATION

### vs Aggro
- **Your plan:** Survive T1-T2, trade efficiently, outlast
- **Key question:** Can you set up before they OHKO you?
- **Tech focus:** Healing, protection, faster attacker
- **Mulligan:** Keep hands with T1 defender or T1 attacker

### vs Control
- **Your plan:** Apply pressure before they stabilize, don't overextend
- **Key question:** Can you close before they run out of resources?
- **Tech focus:** Disruption, faster clock, deck-out protection
- **Mulligan:** Keep hands with consistent setup + early pressure

### vs Midrange
- **Your plan:** Out-tech them, win the tech war
- **Key question:** Who has better sideboard cards?
- **Tech focus:** Specific counters to their tech cards
- **Mulligan:** Keep hands with flexible game plan

### vs Combo
- **Your plan:** Disrupt at the right time, don't let them complete
- **Key question:** When do they need specific pieces?
- **Tech focus:** Hand disruption, item denial, gust their setup
- **Mulligan:** Keep hands with early disruption + pressure

### vs Stall
- **Your plan:** Mill them, pressure them, don't let them stabilize
- **Key question:** Can you deck them out before they deck you out?
- **Tech focus:** Mill effects, alternate win condition
- **Mulligan:** Keep hands with mill pressure or fast clock

## SIDEBOARD STRATEGY (Bo3)

### Game 1
- Play main deck, learn their game plan
- Note their key cards, patterns, weaknesses
- Don't reveal sideboard tech yet

### Games 2-3
- Bring in matchup-specific counters
- Remove cards that don't pull weight vs their deck
- Keep core engine intact
- Don't over-sideboard — stability matters

### Sideboard Principles
- **Don't sideboard for game 1** — you don't know their deck yet
- **Sideboard for THEIR deck, not yours** — bring answers to their threats
- **Keep your engine** — don't remove consistency for narrow tech
- **Don't overdo it** — 4-6 card swaps max usually

## MENTAL GAME

### Tilt Prevention
- Accept variance — Pokémon TCG has luck
- Don't chase losses — each game is independent
- Focus on decisions, not results
- Take breaks between rounds
- Stay hydrated, eat, breathe

### Pressure Management
- Visualize success before matches
- Focus on process, not outcome
- Remember: everyone feels pressure
- Use routine: same warmup, same breathing, same prep

### Recovery
- Loss happens — analyze it, learn from it, move on
- Don't dwell on what you can't change
- Focus on what you CAN control: your decisions, your preparation, your attitude

## ADVANCED TOURNAMENT TIPS

- **Read your opponent** — experienced players telegraph their plan
- **Exploit patterns** — many players have tendencies
- **Manage your clock** — don't let them force you into bad lines
- **Concede strategically** — if game is hopeless, save time for next game
- **Stay humble** — even favorites lose; stay sharp
- **Enjoy the game** — it's supposed to be fun
